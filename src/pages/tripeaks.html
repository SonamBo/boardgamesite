How to play TriPeaks

TriPeaks is a quick, fun solitaire game with three overlapping peaks of cards. Clear them all by building long chains on the waste pile.

  1. The deal. 28 cards form three peaks. The bottom row is face up; the rest turn over as the cards covering them are cleared.
  2. Play one up or one down. Move a face-up card to the waste pile if it’s one rank higher or lower than the top waste card. A 7 can go on a 6 or an 8.
  3. Kings and Aces connect, so an Ace can go on a King and a King on an Ace.
  4. Draw when stuck. Tap the deck to turn a new card onto the waste pile.
  5. Clear all three peaks to win. When the deck runs out and no card fits, the game is over.

Quick tips for clearing the peaks

  • Build long chains. Going up and down lets you play many cards before drawing again.
  • Prefer cards that uncover others. When two cards fit, play the one that turns a new card face up.
  • Look ahead along the chain. 6, 7, 8, 7, 6: zig-zagging can clear more than going one way.
  • Work on all three peaks. The tops are the hardest to reach, so open each peak early.

TriPeaks FAQ

Is TriPeaks free?

Yes. Play as many games as you like in your browser on any device. No download and no account needed.

Do Kings and Aces connect?

Yes. You can play an Ace on a King and a King on an Ace, so chains can wrap around.

Can I go through the deck more than once?

No. Each card in the deck is turned over once. When it runs out and no card fits, the game ends.

What is a chain?

The number of cards you play in a row from the peaks without drawing from the deck. Your longest chain is saved.

Is there an undo?

Yes. Undo takes back as many moves as you like.
