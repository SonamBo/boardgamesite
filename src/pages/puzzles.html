Daily puzzles

One chess, one checkers and one mancala puzzle every day. Solve all three to keep your streak going, then share your result.

How the daily puzzles work

Every day at midnight there’s a new puzzle for each game: a checkmate to find in chess, a winning jump in checkers, a free turn or capture in mancala. Each one comes from our step-by-step learning paths, so if a puzzle stumps you, the lesson it comes from explains the idea.

Get it right first time for a clean ✅, or keep trying: every puzzle can be solved. Your streak counts the days in a row you solve at least one.

New to a game? Start with the Chess path, the Checkers path or the Mancala path.
