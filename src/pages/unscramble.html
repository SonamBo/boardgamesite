How to play Unscramble

Each round has ten words with their letters mixed up. Put the letters back in order to spell the word.

  1. Tap the letters in order. They move up into the answer row. You can type them too.
  2. Changed your mind? Tap a letter in the answer row, or press Backspace, to put it back.
  3. Fill the row to check. If it’s a real word using all the letters, you score. Any correct word counts, even if it’s not the one we picked.
  4. Stuck? Shuffle mixes the letters again, Hint places the next correct letter, and Skip moves on.

Each word is worth 10 points, minus 3 for every hint, down to a minimum of 2. Choose short words (4–5 letters), medium (5–6) or long (7–8).

Quick tips for unscrambling

  • Look for common pairs. TH, CH, SH, ST, ING and ER are often hiding in the jumble.
  • Try the vowels in different spots. Most words alternate between vowels and consonants.
  • Shuffle often. A new order can make the word jump out.
  • Think about endings. -ED, -LY, -ER and -EST are common at the end of longer words.

Unscramble FAQ

Is Unscramble free?

Yes. Play as many rounds as you like, free in your browser on any device. No download and no account needed.

What if I find a different word with the same letters?

It counts. Any real word that uses all the letters is accepted.

How is the score worked out?

Each word is worth 10 points. Every hint takes off 3, down to a minimum of 2 points for a solved word. Skipped words score nothing.

Can I type my answer?

Yes. Type the letters on your keyboard and press Backspace to take one back.

Which words are used?

Everyday English words of the length you choose, picked at random each round.
