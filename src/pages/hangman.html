How to play Hangman

Hangman is the classic pencil-and-paper guessing game. A word is hidden as a row of blanks, and you guess it one letter at a time before the drawing is finished.

  1. Guess a letter. Tap it on the keyboard below the word, or type it.
  2. Right letters appear in every place they occur in the word.
  3. Wrong letters add to the drawing. You can make six wrong guesses: head, body, two arms and two legs.
  4. Fill in the whole word before the drawing is complete to win.

Three ways to play

  • Solo: the computer picks a word and shows its theme as a clue, like Fruit, Space or Music.
  • 2 players on one device: Player 1 types a secret word while Player 2 looks away, then Player 2 guesses.
  • Send a link: type a word and send the link to a friend anywhere. They open it on their own phone or computer and try to guess your word, then they can send one back. No accounts, no app.

Play Hangman with friends online

Choose Send a link, type a word and tap Make link. Share it in a chat, a text or an email. Your friend sees the blanks and the drawing, with no clue but the length of the word, and when they finish they can send you a word straight back. It’s a quick way to play with a friend, a class or a family group chat.

Which letters to guess first

In English, E is the most common letter, followed by letters such as T, A, O, I, N, S, R and H. A good opening is two or three vowels, then the common consonants. Once a few letters are showing, look at the shape: a word ending in a blank after -IN_ is often -ING, and many words end in -ER, -ED or -TION.

  • Use the clue. In Solo, think of words in the theme that fit the number of letters.
  • Count the blanks. Short words are often harder than long ones: there are fewer letters to find and more words that fit.
  • Watch for double letters. One right guess can fill two blanks at once, as in BALLOON.

Hard Hangman words to stump a friend

The toughest words are short and use rare letters, so common guesses miss. Try words like JAZZ, FUZZY, RHYTHM, JINX, QUIZ, KAYAK or ZIGZAG. Words with few vowels or repeated unusual letters make the guesser burn through their six mistakes fast.

A little history

Nobody knows exactly who invented Hangman, but it was a well-known word game by the late 1800s, when it appeared in English books of games. It has stayed popular in classrooms ever since, because it practises spelling and vocabulary while feeling like a game.

Hangman FAQ

Is Hangman free?

Yes. Play as many words as you like, solo or with friends, free in your browser on any phone, tablet or computer. No download and no account needed.

How do I play Hangman online with a friend?

Choose Send a link, type a word and tap Make link, then send the link. Your friend opens it and guesses your word on their own device. To play side by side instead, choose 2 players and pass the device.

How many wrong guesses do I get?

Six. Each wrong letter adds one part to the drawing: head, body, two arms and two legs.

Can my friend see the word in the link?

Not at a glance: the word is scrambled in the link, so it doesn’t show as plain text.

Where do the words come from?

In Solo, words come from themed lists like Animals, Space and Music, and the theme is shown as a clue. In 2 players and Send a link, you choose the word, from our list of everyday English words.

Is Hangman good for kids?

Yes. It’s great spelling practice, and the themed clues in Solo help younger players. Teachers often use it as a quick classroom game.
