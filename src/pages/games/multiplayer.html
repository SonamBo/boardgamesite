2 player games: play a friend online or side by side

Free board and card games for two. Send a link and play on separate devices, or pass one phone back and forth. No download, no sign-up.

Play a friend online in 10 seconds

  1. Pick a game below.
  2. Tap “Create a game link” beside the board.
  3. Send the link by text or chat. The game starts as soon as your friend opens it, on their own phone, tablet or computer.

Each of you sees the board from your own side, moves appear on both screens straight away, and Rematch starts the next game.

2 player games FAQ

Are these 2 player games free?

Yes. Every game is free to play in your browser, as many times as you like, online or on one device.

Do we need an account to play online?

No. Guests can create and join games. Your friend should open the link on their own device, either logged out or with their own account.

Which games can I play online with a friend?

Chess, Checkers, Russian Checkers, Four in a Row, Tic Tac Toe, Reversi, Five in a Row, Go, Hex, Mancala, Nine Men’s Morris, Dots and Boxes and Crazy Eights. More are on the way.

Can we play on one phone?

Yes. Choose “2 players” on any game here and take turns on the same screen.

Do the games work on phones and tablets?

Yes. They work on any phone, tablet or computer with a modern browser, and you and your friend can be on different kinds of device.
