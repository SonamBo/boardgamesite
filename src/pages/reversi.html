How to play

Place a disc so that one or more of the computer’s discs sit in a straight line between your new disc and another of yours, in any direction. Those discs flip to your colour. If you can’t move, your turn is skipped. When neither side can move, whoever has more discs wins. Corners can never be flipped, so they’re worth grabbing.
