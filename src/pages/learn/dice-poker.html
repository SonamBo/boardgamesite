How to play Dice Poker: hand rankings, odds and strategy

Dice Poker is quick to learn: two rolls, five dice, best hand wins. The skill is in what you keep.

Try these ideas in a game to three.

The rules

Each round, you and your opponent each roll five dice. After your first roll, keep any dice you like and roll the rest once more, or stand. The best poker hand wins the round. First to win three rounds wins the game.

Hand rankings

  1. Five of a kind
  2. Four of a kind
  3. Full house: three of one number and two of another
  4. High straight: 2-3-4-5-6
  5. Low straight: 1-2-3-4-5
  6. Three of a kind
  7. Two pair
  8. One pair
  9. Nothing

When both hands are the same type, the higher numbers win: three 6s beat three 5s.

What to keep

  • Three of a kind: keep them and roll two dice for four of a kind or a full house.
  • Two pair: keep both pairs and roll one die for a full house.
  • One pair: keep the pair and roll three.
  • Four in a row: keep them and roll one die for a straight, but only if you have nothing better.
  • Nothing: keep the highest die, or roll all five.

When to stand

If you roll second, you know what you have to beat. Stand as soon as your hand wins. If you roll first, stand on a full house or better.

The odds of each hand

These are the chances of rolling each hand with five dice in a single roll:

  • Five of a kind: 0.08% (about 1 in 1,300)
  • Four of a kind: 1.9%
  • Full house: 3.9%
  • High straight: 1.5%
  • Low straight: 1.5%
  • Three of a kind: 15.4%
  • Two pair: 23.1%
  • One pair: 46.3%
  • Nothing: 6.2%

Notice that a straight is rarer than a full house on one roll, but straights rank lower. That’s the traditional order, kept from card poker.

Why keeping a pair beats chasing a straight

Holding a pair and rolling three dice gives you three chances to improve, to three of a kind, two pair or better. Holding four to a straight and rolling one die only works if that die is the one number you need: a 1 in 3 chance at best. Unless you need a straight to win the round, keep the pair.

Dice Poker questions

What is the order of hands in poker dice?

From best to worst: five of a kind, four of a kind, full house, high straight, low straight, three of a kind, two pair, one pair, nothing.

Does a straight beat a full house in Dice Poker?

No. In the traditional ranking a full house beats both straights, even though a straight is slightly rarer on one roll.

How many times can you roll in Dice Poker?

Twice. After your first roll you can keep any dice and roll the rest once more, or stand on what you have.

What happens if both players have the same hand?

The hand with the higher numbers wins: three 6s beat three 5s, and in a full house the three of a kind is compared first.

Is Dice Poker the same as Yahtzee?

No. Both use five dice, but Dice Poker is a head-to-head game of best hands over two rolls, while Yahtzee-style games fill in a scorecard over three rolls a turn. On BoardGameSite the scorecard game is Five Dice.
