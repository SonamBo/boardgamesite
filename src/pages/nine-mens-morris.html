How to play Nine Men’s Morris

Nine Men’s Morris is one of the oldest board games, played for over two thousand years. Each player has nine pieces and a board of 24 points.

  1. Place. Take turns placing one piece on any empty point until all 18 pieces are placed.
  2. Move. Then take turns moving a piece along a line to a neighbouring empty point.
  3. Make a mill. Three of your pieces in a straight line along a line of the board is a mill. Each time you make one, remove one of your opponent’s pieces. Pieces in a mill can’t be taken unless all their pieces are in mills.
  4. Fly. When a player is down to three pieces, they can move a piece to any empty point.
  5. Win by reducing your opponent to two pieces, or leaving them with no legal move.

Quick tips for winning

  • Don’t rush mills while placing. Flexible positions on the busy middle points are worth more early on.
  • Build a running mill. A mill you can open and close by moving one piece back and forth takes a piece every other turn.
  • Block two-in-a-rows before they become mills.
  • Keep your pieces mobile. Being blocked in is a common way to lose.

Nine Men’s Morris FAQ

Is Nine Men’s Morris free?

Yes. Play the computer or a friend as often as you like, free in your browser.

What is a mill?

Three of your pieces in a row along one of the board’s lines. Making a mill lets you remove an opponent’s piece.

Can I remove a piece that’s in a mill?

Only if every one of your opponent’s pieces is in a mill.

What is flying?

When a player has only three pieces left, they can move a piece to any empty point, not just a neighbouring one.

How old is the game?

Very old: boards have been found carved into ancient Roman buildings, and it was popular across medieval Europe.
