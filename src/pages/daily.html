How the daily challenge works

    
Every day at midnight, three games are picked for the day’s challenge. It could be a chess, checkers or mancala puzzle that you solve right here, or today’s Sudoku, Word Guess, Letter Hive or Four in a Row position.

    
The board puzzles come from our step-by-step learning paths, so if one stumps you, the lesson it comes from explains the idea. Your streak counts the days in a row you complete at least one challenge.
