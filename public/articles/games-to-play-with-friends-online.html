Free online games to play with friends

Want a quick game with a friend who isn’t in the room? These games run in your browser: start one, send the link, and you’re playing together in seconds. No downloads, no accounts, and they work on phones, tablets and computers.

How it works

  1. Open a game from the list below.
  2. Tap “Create a game link” in the panel beside the board.
  3. Send the link by text, chat or email. When your friend opens it, the game starts on both screens.

When the game ends, tap Rematch to go again. Guests can play without signing up.

Quick games (under 5 minutes)

  • Tic Tac Toe: the fastest game there is, and still fun to win. Perfect for a quick break.
  • Four in a Row: drop discs and line up four before your friend does. Easy to learn, surprisingly tricky.
  • Hangman: type a secret word and send it as a link. Your friend guesses it on their own phone, then sends one back.

Card games

  • Crazy Eights: the classic card game that inspired Uno. Match the suit or number, play eights to change the suit, and be first to empty your hand. Each of you sees only your own cards.

Strategy games

  • Chess: the great game of strategy, with every rule included.
  • Checkers: jump, crown kings and clear the board.
  • Reversi: trap your friend’s discs to flip them. The lead can change on the very last move.
  • Five in a Row: also called Gomoku. Get five stones in a line on a big board.
  • Go: the ancient game of territory, played on a beginner-friendly 9×9 board.
  • Hex: connect your two sides of the board. There are never any draws.

Questions

Do my friend and I both need an account?

No. Anyone can create a game link or join one as a guest. A free account only adds your record and streaks across devices.

Do we need to download anything?

No. Everything runs in the browser on phones, tablets and computers. You can be on different kinds of device.

How many players can join an online game?

Online games are for two players: the person who makes the link and the friend who opens it.

What are good online games to play with friends for free?

For something quick, Tic Tac Toe, Four in a Row or Hangman. For a card game, Crazy Eights. For a longer, deeper game, Chess, Reversi or Go.

Can we play again after a game ends?

Yes. Tap Rematch and your friend gets the new game straight away.
