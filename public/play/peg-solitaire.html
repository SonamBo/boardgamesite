How to play

Jump a peg over its neighbour into an empty hole, straight across or up and down. The peg you jump over is removed. Finish with one peg to win.

How to play Peg Solitaire

Peg Solitaire is one of the oldest puzzles in the world. You start with 32 pegs on a cross-shaped board of 33 holes, with only the centre hole empty.

  1. Jump a peg. Pick a peg and jump it over a peg right next to it, into an empty hole directly beyond. Jumps go left, right, up or down, never diagonally.
  2. The jumped peg is removed. Every jump takes one peg off the board.
  3. Keep jumping. If the peg you just moved can jump again, it stays selected so you can chain jumps.
  4. The game ends when no jump is possible. Leave just one peg to win, and finish in the centre hole for a perfect game.

Pegs that can jump are lit. Tap one to see where it can go. Undo takes back as many jumps as you like.

Quick tips for finishing with one peg

  • Don’t strand pegs in the corners. A peg alone in one of the four arms is very hard to rescue later.
  • Clear one arm at a time. Work methodically rather than jumping wherever you can.
  • Keep pegs together. A peg needs a neighbour to jump over, so scattered pegs are stuck pegs.
  • Save the centre for the end. Bring the last pegs back toward the middle.

Peg Solitaire FAQ

Can Peg Solitaire always be solved?

Yes. On the English board, starting with only the centre empty, it’s possible to finish with a single peg, even in the centre hole. It just takes careful planning.

How many pegs are there?

The English board has 33 holes. You start with 32 pegs and the centre hole empty.

Can pegs jump diagonally?

No. On the English board, pegs only jump left, right, up or down.

Is there an undo?

Yes. You can undo every jump back to the start of the game.

Is Peg Solitaire free?

Yes. Play as many games as you like in your browser on any device. No download and no account needed.
