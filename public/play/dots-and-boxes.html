How to play Dots and Boxes

Dots and Boxes is a classic pencil-and-paper game, simple to learn and full of clever traps. Against the computer, choose You play: First, Second or Random to set who draws the first line.

  1. Take turns drawing one line between two dots next to each other, across or down.
  2. Close a box by drawing its fourth side, and it’s yours.
  3. Closing a box earns another turn, so you can sometimes take several in a row.
  4. When every line is drawn, the player with the most boxes wins.

Quick tips for winning

  • Don’t draw the third side of a box. It hands your opponent that box.
  • Count the chains. Late in the game, the board splits into chains of boxes. When you must give one away, give the shortest.
  • The double-cross. Experts sometimes leave the last two boxes of a long chain for their opponent, so that the opponent has to open the next chain.

Dots and Boxes FAQ

Is Dots and Boxes free?

Yes. Play the computer or a friend as often as you like, free in your browser.

Can I play Dots and Boxes online with a friend?

Yes. Tap Create a game link beside the board and send the link. Your friend opens it on their own phone, tablet or computer and you play the same game together. To play side by side instead, choose 2 players.

Can I take back a move?

Yes. Undo takes back your last move, and against the computer its reply too. Undo isn’t available in online games.

Do I get another turn after closing a box?

Yes. Every time you complete a box, you draw another line.

What board sizes can I play?

3×3, 4×4, 5×5 or 6×6 boxes. Bigger boards take longer and have more room for strategy.

How good is the computer?

Easy sometimes misses boxes and gives them away. Medium never hands you a box if it can help it. Hard also keeps its losses as small as possible at the end.

Can it be a draw?

Yes, on boards with an even number of boxes, if both players finish with the same number.
