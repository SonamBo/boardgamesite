How to play Dice Poker

Dice Poker plays like a quick hand of poker, with five dice instead of cards. Each round, both players roll, and the better hand wins. First to three rounds wins the game.

  1. Roll all five dice.
  2. Keep the dice you want by tapping them, then roll the rest once more. Or stand with what you have.
  3. Compare hands. The higher hand wins the round. If the hands are the same type, the higher numbers win.

The hands, best first

  • Five of a kind: all five dice the same.
  • Four of a kind: four the same.
  • Full house: three of one number and two of another.
  • High straight: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.
  • Low straight: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.
  • Three of a kind: three the same.
  • Two pair: two different pairs.
  • One pair: two the same.
  • Nothing: no matches and no straight. Highest dice decide.

Quick tips for better hands

  • Keep your biggest group. With a pair or three of a kind, roll the other dice for more of the same.
  • Chase a straight carefully. Keep four in a row only when you have nothing better.
  • Know when to stand. If you go second and already beat your opponent, stop.

Dice Poker FAQ

Is Dice Poker free?

Yes. Play the computer or a friend as often as you like, free in your browser.

How many times can I roll?

Twice per round: once with all five dice, then once more with any dice you didn’t keep.

What beats what?

Five of a kind, four of a kind, full house, high straight, low straight, three of a kind, two pair, one pair, then nothing. Ties of the same hand are decided by the higher numbers.

Is this the same as Yahtzee or Liar’s Dice?

No. Dice Poker is a straight showdown of poker hands. For a scorecard game try Five Dice, and for bluffing try Liar’s Dice.

How good is the computer?

Easy keeps any matches it rolls. Medium and Hard also chase straights and know when to stand.
