How to play Slide & Merge

Slide & Merge is a number puzzle on a 4×4 board. Every move slides all the tiles as far as they’ll go in one direction, and matching tiles join together.

  1. Slide the tiles. Swipe on the board, use the arrow keys, or tap the arrows under it. Every tile moves as far as it can that way.
  2. Merge matching numbers. When two tiles with the same number touch as they slide, they merge into one tile worth both: two 8s make a 16.
  3. A new tile appears after every move, a 2 or occasionally a 4, in a random empty square.
  4. Reach 2048 to win. Then keep going for a bigger tile and a higher score if you like.
  5. The game ends when the board is full and no tiles can merge.

Every merge adds the new tile’s value to your score. Made a slip? Undo takes back your last move.

Quick tips for a higher score

  • Pick a corner and keep your biggest tile in it. Most strong players use a bottom corner.
  • Use mostly two or three directions. If your big tile is bottom-left, slide down and left as much as you can, and avoid up.
  • Build a chain along one edge. Line tiles up in order, like 256, 128, 64, 32, so each merge flows into the next.
  • Don’t chase small merges. A tidy board matters more than a few points now.

Slide & Merge FAQ

Is Slide & Merge the same as 2048?

Yes. It plays by the same rules as the popular 2048 puzzle: slide the tiles, merge matching numbers, and aim for a 2048 tile.

Can I keep playing after 2048?

Yes. When you make 2048 you can choose Keep going and play on for 4096 and beyond, as long as you have moves left.

Is there an undo?

Yes. You can undo your last move once before making another.

Will my game be saved?

Yes. Your board and score are saved in your browser, so you can close the page and carry on later.

Is Slide & Merge free?

Yes. Play as often as you like in your browser on a phone, tablet or computer. No download and no account needed.
