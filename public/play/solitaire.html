How to play

Move all 52 cards onto the four foundations at the top right, building each suit up from Ace to King. In the seven columns, stack cards downwards in alternating colours (a red 6 on a black 7). You can move a whole run of face-up cards together, and only a King can go into an empty column. When you’re stuck, draw from the deck.

Draw 1 or Draw 3?

Draw 1 turns over one card at a time and wins far more often, which makes it good for a relaxed game. Draw 3 is the classic, harder version: only the top card of each three is playable.
