How to play Crazy Eights

Crazy Eights is a fast, friendly card game played with an ordinary deck, and the classic that inspired Uno and many other shedding games. Be the first to get rid of all your cards.

Play Crazy Eights online with a friend

Tap Create a game link and send the link to a friend. When they open it, you’re playing the same game on two devices: you each see only your own hand, and the draw pile and discards are shared. No account or download needed. Prefer to play solo? Take on one, two or three computer players at three levels.

  1. The deal. With two players, each gets 7 cards; with three or four, 5 each. One card is turned face up to start the discard pile.
  2. Match the top card by suit (a heart on a heart) or by number (a 9 on a 9).
  3. Eights are wild. Play an 8 on anything, then choose the suit the next player must follow.
  4. Can’t play? Draw. Take one card from the pile. If you can play it, you may; otherwise, pass.
  5. First to play their last card wins.

Crazy Eights and Uno

If you’ve played Uno, you already know how Crazy Eights works: match the colour (here, the suit) or the number, play a wild card to change it, and race to empty your hand. Uno was created in 1971 as a version of Crazy Eights with its own special deck. Crazy Eights needs only a standard pack of cards, and the eights do the job of Uno’s wild cards.

Quick tips for winning

  • Save your eights. They get you out of trouble, so don’t waste them early.
  • Steer the suit. When you play an 8, pick the suit you hold most of.
  • Change number to change suit. Playing a 6 of clubs on a 6 of hearts switches the suit without using an 8.
  • Watch who’s nearly out. When someone has one card left, try to move to a suit they probably can’t follow.

Crazy Eights FAQ

Is Crazy Eights free?

Yes. Play as many games as you like against one, two or three computer players, or online with a friend, free in your browser.

How do I play Crazy Eights online with friends?

Tap Create a game link and send the link. Your friend opens it on their phone or computer and the game starts. Online games are for two players.

Can I play an 8 at any time?

Yes. An 8 can go on any card, and then you choose the suit for the next player.

What happens if I can’t play?

Draw one card from the pile. If it fits, you can play it; if not, tap Pass and the next player goes.

What if the draw pile runs out?

The discard pile, except its top card, is shuffled to make a new draw pile.

Is this the same as Uno?

Not quite. Crazy Eights is the traditional card game that Uno was based on, played with an ordinary deck instead of Uno’s special cards. If you like Uno, you’ll feel at home straight away. (UNO is a trademark of Mattel; BoardGameSite isn’t connected with Mattel.)
