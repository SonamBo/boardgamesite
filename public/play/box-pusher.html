How to play Box Pusher

Box Pusher is a warehouse puzzle. You’re the keeper, and your job is to push every box onto one of the target squares.

  1. Walk around. Move one square at a time with the arrow keys, by swiping on the board, or with the arrows below it.
  2. Push boxes. Walk into a box to push it one square. You can only push, never pull.
  3. One at a time. You can’t push a box into a wall or into another box.
  4. Cover every target to finish the level. Boxes turn gold when they’re on a target.

There are 30 levels, getting harder as you go, from two boxes up to four. Solve a level to open the next one. Undo takes back moves one at a time, and Restart puts the level back to the start.

Quick tips for tricky levels

  • Never push a box into a corner unless that corner is a target. A box in a corner can never be moved again.
  • Watch the walls. A box pushed against a wall can only slide along that wall, so make sure there’s a target on it.
  • Plan the order. Fill the targets furthest from the door first, so earlier boxes don’t block the later ones.
  • Count pushes, not steps. After each level you’ll see the fewest pushes possible, a good target for a second try.

Box Pusher FAQ

Is Box Pusher the same as Sokoban?

It follows the same classic rules as Sokoban-style puzzles: push the boxes onto the targets, never pull. The 30 levels here were made for BoardGameSite.

Can every level be solved?

Yes. Every level was checked with a solver, which also worked out the fewest pushes needed.

What if I get stuck?

Use Undo to step back, or Restart to put the level back to the start. A box in a corner or against the wrong wall usually means it’s time to undo.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. The levels you’ve solved and the one you’re playing are saved in your browser.

Is Box Pusher free?

Yes. Play all 30 levels free in your browser on any device. No download and no account needed.
