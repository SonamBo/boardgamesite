How to play Hangman

Hangman is the classic guessing game. A word is hidden as a row of blanks, and you guess it one letter at a time.

  1. Guess a letter. Tap it on the keyboard below the word, or type it.
  2. Right letters appear in every place they occur in the word.
  3. Wrong letters add to the drawing. You can make six wrong guesses: head, body, two arms and two legs.
  4. Fill in the whole word before the drawing is complete to win.

In Solo, the computer picks a word and gives you its theme as a clue, like Fruit or Space. In 2 players, Player 1 types a secret word while Player 2 looks away, then Player 2 guesses.

Quick tips for guessing right

  • Start with vowels. Nearly every word has an A, E, I, O or U.
  • Then common consonants. R, S, T, L and N appear in lots of words.
  • Use the clue. Think of words in the theme that fit the number of letters.
  • Look at the shape. Once a few letters are in, endings like -ING, -ER or -TION often jump out.

Hangman FAQ

Is Hangman free?

Yes. Play as many words as you like, solo or with a friend, free in your browser. No download and no account needed.

How many wrong guesses do I get?

Six. Each wrong letter adds one part to the drawing: head, body, two arms and two legs.

How do I play with a friend?

Choose 2 players. Player 1 types a secret word while Player 2 looks away, then hands over the device for Player 2 to guess.

Where do the words come from?

In Solo, words come from themed lists like Animals, Space and Music, and the theme is shown as a clue.

Can I use my keyboard?

Yes. Type any letter to guess it.
