Free classroom games for teachers

Logic puzzles, maths games, strategy board games and word games that run in any browser, including school Chromebooks. Students don’t need an account, and there’s nothing to install. Each game below comes with what it practises and a quick way to use it in class.

Who this page is for: teachers of students aged 13 and over (middle school, secondary and high school, after-school clubs, adult and ESL classes). BoardGameSite is a general-audience site, so for younger children please use it teacher-led, on a shared screen.

What students need

  • A browser. Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox on a Chromebook, laptop, tablet or phone.
  • No accounts. Every game can be played without signing up or giving any personal information.
  • No ads. We don’t show advertising.
  • A link. Send students straight to a game, for example boardgamesite.com/play/tower-of-hanoi. Each game remembers progress in the student’s own browser.

Privacy, in plain words

  • Students can play every game without an account and without entering any personal information.
  • We use Google Analytics only if the visitor accepts cookies. If they decline, it isn’t loaded.
  • Scores and progress are saved in the student’s own browser unless they choose to create an account.
  • There’s no advertising, and we don’t sell information.

The full details are in our privacy policy. Questions from schools are welcome: contact us.

Questions from teachers

Is BoardGameSite free for schools?

Yes. Every game and lesson is free, with no limits and no school licence.

Do the games work on Chromebooks and iPads?

Yes. Everything runs in the browser, with touch and mouse controls. Nothing needs installing.

Can students play each other?

Yes: on one shared device by choosing “2 players”, or on two devices by sharing a game link.

What age are the games suitable for?

The games themselves suit most ages, but the site is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over. With younger classes, run the games from the teacher’s screen.

Can I link to a game from Google Classroom or our school website?

Yes, please do. Each game has its own simple link, such as boardgamesite.com/play/sudoku.
