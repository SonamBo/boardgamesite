How checkers pieces move

A checkers piece moves one square diagonally forward onto an empty dark square. Learn how pieces move with practice on a real board. How checkers works · lesson 2 of 6.

The lesson in words

A piece moves one square diagonally forward, onto an empty dark square.

Ordinary pieces (called men) can’t move backwards or sideways.

Your piece on d4 has two choices.

Try it: Tap every square this piece can move to.

A piece on the edge has only one forward diagonal.

Try it: Tap every square this piece can move to.

Your turn. Tap your piece, then the square to move it to.

Try it: Move your piece forward.

One step, diagonally, forwards. Next: jumping, which is how you capture.
