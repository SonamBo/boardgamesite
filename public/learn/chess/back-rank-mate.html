The back rank mate in chess

The back rank mate traps a king behind its own pawns. Learn to deliver it and to protect your own king from it. Checkmate patterns · lesson 1 of 3.

The lesson in words

A king that has castled often sits behind three pawns. That’s safe, until a rook or queen reaches the back rank. The pawns become a cage.

Black’s king has no escape square.

Questions

Would Rd8+ still be mate?

No. That little pawn move gave the king a window on h7. Making one is the easiest way to stop back rank mates.
