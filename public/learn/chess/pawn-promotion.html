Pawn promotion in chess

A pawn that reaches the far side of the board is promoted to a queen, rook, bishop or knight. Learn the rule and promote your first pawn. Special moves · lesson 3 of 3.

The lesson in words

When a pawn reaches the far side of the board, it promotes: you swap it for a queen, rook, bishop or knight of your colour.

Almost everyone picks a queen. You can have two or more queens at once.

Here the lesson board promotes to a queen for you.

Questions

Which piece can a pawn not promote to?

A king. You only ever have one king.
