Questions

Which is true about castling?

The king moves two squares and the rook hops over it. Once the king has moved, castling is gone for good.

What’s the result?

Stalemate. Black isn’t in check but has no legal move, so it’s a draw.

A pawn moves two squares and lands beside your pawn. When can you capture it en passant?

Only on the very next move.
