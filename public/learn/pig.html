How to play Pig: the hold-at-20 rule

Pig has one decision: roll again, or hold. Making that decision well is the whole game.

Try the rule against the computer.

Play Pig free

The rules

On your turn, roll one die as often as you like, adding up the numbers. Roll a 1 and you lose everything from that turn. Hold to bank your turn total and pass the die. First to 100 wins.

Hold at 20

Each roll has a 1 in 6 chance of a 1, which costs your whole turn total. The average gain from a good roll is 4 points. When your turn total reaches about 20, the expected loss (20 × 1/6, about 3.3) starts to match the expected gain (4 × 5/6, about 3.3). So holding at around 20 is a strong simple strategy.

When to break the rule

  • Close to 100: if a smaller amount wins the game, hold as soon as you reach 100.
  • Way behind: if your opponent is near 100, banking 20 won’t be enough. Keep rolling for a big turn.
  • Way ahead: you can hold a little earlier and play safe.

A game for learning

Pig is often used in classrooms to teach probability. Try playing a few games holding at 10, then 20, then 30, and see which wins most.
