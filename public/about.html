About BoardGameSite

BoardGameSite is a place to play board games in your browser: no download, no account and no fuss. Pick a game, play a few rounds against the computer, and come back tomorrow for a fresh daily challenge.

We’re starting with Solitaire, Checkers, Reversi and Four in a Row, and adding more strategy, card, dice and puzzle games over time. Accounts, leaderboards and playing against friends online are next on the list.

Who makes it

BoardGameSite is made by Pixel Art Games, a small independent game studio. You can watch game previews and daily challenge solutions on our YouTube channel.
